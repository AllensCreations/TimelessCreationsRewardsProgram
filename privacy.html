This Privacy Policy applies to all interactions with the TCRP automated Messenger service, webhooks, and associated administrative tools. By using TCRP, you consent to the collection, processing, and storage practices described herein.

To provide rewards tracking, account verification, and program management, we collect limited personal information, including:

Your information is used strictly for technical operation and account administration:

  • To maintain application security, prevent unauthorized duplicate registrations, and manage administrative privileges.

4. Data Storage & Third-Party Services

We implement industry-standard encryption and security measures. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share data only with verified infrastructure providers required to run this application:

  • Google Firebase Firestore: Cloud database infrastructure for secure real-time state and account storage.
  • Vercel Cloud Infrastructure: Serverless deployment and execution host for Messenger Webhook API endpoints.
  • Brevo (formerly Sendinblue): Transactional email service used exclusively for dispatching verification codes.
  • Meta Graph API: For receiving and responding to Facebook Messenger communications.

5. Data Retention & User Rights

We retain account records for as long as your account remains active within the rewards program. You have the right to request access to or deletion of your personal data at any time.

In compliance with Meta Developer Policies, if you wish to revoke access or request full deletion of your user profile, PSID records, and earned points balance from our databases, you may do so by:

  • Sending a direct message containing /delete_account in our Messenger chat.
