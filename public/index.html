Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about points & rewards

How do I earn TCRP Points?▼
Every missionary starts with 2 welcome points! You earn +2 points for every new missionary who joins TCRP using your unique referral code (e.g. TCRP-7721).
How do I redeem my items?▼
Click the "Redeem via Messenger" button on any item in the Catalog tab, or send a direct message to m.me/TimelessCreationsRP with your referral code.
What is "Gawa Muna Bago Bayad"?▼
For custom orders outside points redemption, we craft your customized wooden nametag or scripture case first, show you the photo proofs, and you only pay once satisfied!
